Andrix

Instructions

The objective of Andrix is to fence off a majority of the playfield (threshold at the top of the screen). At the start of each level, the playing field is a large, empty rectangle, containing a metal ball, which is the enemy.

The player controls the small diamond-shaped marker that can move around the edges of the rectangle, with the goal to claim as much of the screen as possible via drawing lines. When the player completes a closed shape, the captured area becomes solid and he gets points. To complete a level, the player must claim at least the percentage that is shown as threshold on the top auf the screen!

In expert-mode there are enemies on the outline of the area which kill the player if they touch him.

Controls

You can control the player via d-pad, trackball or touchscreen (swiping) and move it around the sideline of the area or inside. As soon as you reach the sideline the area that you fenced off will get solid. You are not allowed to touch your own line before you reach the sideline!

On a computer: arrow keys or WASD to move, Enter or Space to confirm, Esc for the menu. You can also drag with the mouse.
