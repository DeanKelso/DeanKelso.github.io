+------------------------( ABOUT )----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Hi, I'm Dean

I'm a digital creator and designer from Hillsboro, Oregon. For the past 6+ years I've taken on whatever a project needed: 3D characters and props in Blender, game systems in Unreal Engine 5 and Godot, thumbnails and branding in Photoshop, and video edits in DaVinci Resolve.

I've worked with 10+ clients, made all the art for a game jam game in under 4 days, and brought back an old Kindle game my girlfriend loved as a kid. When something breaks, I dig until I know why.

My rule is simple: if I don't know how to do something yet, I learn it, then I build it. Right now that means three game prototypes, including 1v1 Me-Bro, a competitive party game I'm playtesting with friends, plus an incremental mobile game.
