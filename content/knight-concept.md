Description:
I created this concept piece as a personal desktop background and a sandbox to push a few specific technical skills. My main goals were to practice realistic cloth simulation and sculpting, refine fine facial details like eyes and lashes, and use light linking to craft dedicated eye highlights and dynamic scene lighting.
