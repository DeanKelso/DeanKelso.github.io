Description:
I wanted to experiment with creative shader setups and Sculpting\Modeling an anime inspired character, which naturally steered the concept toward a cyberpunk streetwear aesthetic. As a personal challenge, I built the entire scene in black and white first to lock in the lighting and values before adding color at the end. It was also a great exercise in working smarter across tools shifting heavy color adjustments into Photoshop instead of spending hours tweaking nodes in Blender, which really let the hair sculpt and stylized materials shine.
