Description:
Created in under a week for a Pwnisher 3D community render challenge, this piece was all about rapid execution, strong shot composition, and building an immersive dark fantasy atmosphere. To bring the environment to life on a tight deadline, I built three custom physics systems: an emissive ember system using leaf falling physics, a varied ash particle system, and dual wind/cloth simulations for the background flags and character cloak. Beyond the visuals, I handled the full sound design in DaVinci Resolve and Soundly to sync with an original score composed by a friend.
