Description:
This was a huge undertaking involving over 100 source clips and roughly 100GB of footage. I built a complex custom thumbnail using a mix of ripped game assets, custom character posing, and detailed Photoshop composition. To give the edit a distinct look, I created a custom pipeline that fed textures into ASCII art generator scripts to handle time travel effects and stylized gore instead of standard blood. The background was fully modeled in 3D with custom shaders then polished with digital painting and photobashing, all backed by tight clip pacing, custom sound design, tailored captions, and A/B tested titles and thumbnails.
