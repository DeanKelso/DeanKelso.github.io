Description:
Created in just 3.5 days with a three-person team for the GMTK Game Jam 2026 (theme: "Countdown"), I owned the complete visual identity including all game art and a custom typeface for our deck-building roguelike. To capture a playful, hand-drawn sketchbook aesthetic at game jam speed, I built a modular 2D/3D hybrid pipeline in Blender. By layering Grease Pencil strokes over modified 3D primitives and pairing them with a light-reactive halftone/hatching shader, I automated surface shading and cut down manual drawing time.
