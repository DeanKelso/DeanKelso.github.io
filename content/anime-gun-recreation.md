Title: Anime Gun Recreation
Type: Character, animation
For: Personal
Tools: Blender, Dabinchi resolve
Year: 2024
Featured: yes
OK to show: yes

Description:
Inspired by an anime clip that caught my eye while I was practicing hard surface modeling, I set out to recreate the scene's distinctive 2D aesthetic in 3D. I modeled the Glock to close real world accuracy, pairing it with quickly sculpted hands. To capture the authentic feel of traditional animation, I combined the hand painted textures with procedural shaders for dynamic lighting, then blocking out the animation on 2s and 4s.
