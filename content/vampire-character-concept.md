Description:
A vampire main character concept for a short story. Concept is based on Little Red Riding hood but with a chatholic church storyline + vampire Wild West, and other somewhat steampunky. The story is about her turning into a vampire as a kid then later finding a kid just like her that was turned as well, and trys to protect her from the church and vampire hunters.

I like the face and hair sculpt on this character concept along with the cloak.
