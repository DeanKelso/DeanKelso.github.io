Description:
A custom wine bottle label and logo design created for a home winemaker making bespoke bottles to share with friends and family. The core concept focused on hand drawn scientific compounds, which led to a collaborative back and forth process exploring molecular structures, drawing styles, and visual legibility. I am especially proud of the final logo execution, the organic hand drawn elements, and the subtle easter eggs hidden throughout the design. Balancing traditional vector art with loose illustration gave the piece a distinct, personal character.
