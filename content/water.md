Description:
I originally started this piece just to practice character hair and sculpting, but liked the foundation enough to turn it into a playground for stylized shaders and complex lighting. The centerpiece of the environment is a custom water setup built across three distinct meshes and materials to capture refracted light, caustics, and stylized ripples. From there, I dialed in the skin materials with a subtle halftone texture and used isolated eye lights alongside two sun lamps to create a crisp shadow terminator across the face and body.
